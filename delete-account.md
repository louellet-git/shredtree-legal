# Delete your ShredTree account

*ShredTree, by Luc Ouellet.*

You do not need the app to delete your account.

## How to ask

Email **ouellet.luc1@gmail.com** from the address you sign in with, with the subject
"Delete my ShredTree account". We reply to that address to confirm it is
yours, then schedule the deletion and tell you the date.

## When it happens

Your account is deleted 30 days after you ask. Until then nothing is deleted:
your claims are hidden from other riders, you cannot upload videos, and your
votes and reviews still count. To keep your account, sign in to the app before
then and choose Keep my account. After the 30 days it cannot be undone. In
the app you can instead choose Delete now: your account is deleted at once,
and that cannot be undone.

## What is deleted

Your email address, sign-in session records (IP address, user agent), account
ID, sport declarations, videos, claims, votes, reviews, flags, reports, blocks
and event log entries.

## What survives, and for how long

The sign-in log may keep entries naming your email address and IP address,
with no set end date. The operator's free-text notes of reminders sent may
name you; they are not deleted with your account and are kept
with no set end date. The weekly encrypted database backup holds everything
above except videos, emails included. Each backup is deleted 90 days after it
is made, but each weekly backup copies the sign-in log and those notes again.
