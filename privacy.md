# ShredTree privacy policy

*Operator: Luc Ouellet. Contact: ouellet.luc1@gmail.com. Effective: 2026-10-01.*

ShredTree lets riders prove tricks on video and have other riders verify them.
This page says what we keep, why, who holds it, and what survives when you
delete your account.

## What we keep

- **Email address** — to send your sign-in codes.
- **Sign-in records** — your IP address and user agent for each sign-in
  session, and a sign-in log that can name your email address and IP address.
- **Account ID** — the random ID your account is known by.
- **Sport declarations** — the sports you say you ride.
- **Videos** — the clips you upload, stored as your phone saved them, including
  any metadata inside the file (some phones record location in videos).
- **Claims** — each trick you claim, the clip it points at, and whether the
  claim is public or private.
- **Votes and reviews** — what you cast on other riders' claims.
- **Flags** — disputes you raise on a claim's verification.
- **Reports** — claims or riders you report, and the reason you pick.
- **Blocks** — riders you block.
- **Moderation records** — whether your account is banned, whether a claim of
  yours is hidden, and whether a report you made was reviewed.
- **Event log** — a timestamped record of actions in the app (for example, a
  claim made or withdrawn), keyed to your account ID.
- **Nudge log** — the operator's own free-text notes of reminders sent, which
  may name you.

## Why

To run ShredTree: sign you in, show your progress, let riders verify claims,
and moderate content. And to measure whether ShredTree is working (for
example, how many riders get tricks verified without being reminded), from the
event log and the nudge log. We do not sell your data or use it for
advertising.

## Who sees it

- A **private** claim and its clip: signed-in riders who have its code (you
  choose whom to give it to). A **public** claim and its clip: signed-in riders.
- Votes are secret: no rider sees who voted which way. Flags and reviews are
  not anonymous: the rider who made the claim can read them and the account ID
  that made them.
- The operator, to run and moderate the service.
- Service providers that hold it for us: Supabase (database and sign-in),
  Brevo (sends sign-in emails), Cloudflare (videos and encrypted backups) and
  GitHub (runs the backup job). Those the app connects to also see technical
  data such as your IP address. Supabase, Brevo and Cloudflare process your
  data only to run ShredTree, and their data-processing agreements bind them to
  protect it at least as well as this policy does. GitHub runs the backup job on
  its computers under its terms of service: your data passes through the job
  while the backup is made and encrypted, and the backup is stored with
  Cloudflare.

## Deleting your account

See the [account deletion page](delete-account.html). Your account is deleted
30 days after you ask, or at once if you choose Delete now in the app. Until then your claims are hidden from other riders, and
signing in to the app lets you keep the account. After that, everything listed
above is deleted, including your videos, except what follows. **What survives:** the
sign-in log may keep entries naming your email address and IP address,
with no set end date; the nudge log is not deleted with your account and is
kept with no set end date; and the weekly encrypted database backup holds
everything above except videos — emails included. Each backup is deleted 90
days after it is made, but each weekly backup copies the sign-in log and the
nudge log again.

## Children

ShredTree is for people aged 13 and over. If a child under 13 has an account,
email ouellet.luc1@gmail.com and we will delete it.
